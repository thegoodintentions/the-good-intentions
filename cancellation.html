Booking terms

Cancellation & Postponement

We aim to make our booking arrangements clear, fair and straightforward.

We understand plans can change

We know that sometimes circumstances change and wedding plans don't always go as expected. If you need to cancel or postpone your booking, please contact The Good Intentions in writing as soon as possible.

Cancellations

Any cancellation charge or refund will be assessed fairly, taking into account:

  • How much notice is provided before the event.
  • Costs already incurred that cannot reasonably be recovered.
  • Musician and supplier commitments made for your event.
  • Any reasonable loss arising from the cancellation, including whether the date can be rebooked.

We will consider the circumstances of each cancellation and explain any amount proposed to be retained or refunded.

Postponements & date changes

If you need to move your event to a new date, we'll do our best to accommodate the change, subject to availability. Any revised date, lineup changes or additional costs will be discussed and confirmed in writing.

Unforeseen circumstances

If circumstances outside either party's reasonable control affect the event, we'll work with you in good faith to discuss practical alternatives, including rescheduling where possible.

Please note: This page is a summary of our approach. The terms in your individual booking agreement will apply and are subject to Australian Consumer Law. Any cancellation amounts will be reasonable and assessed in the circumstances.

Need to discuss a change?

Please get in touch as early as possible so we can work through the options with you.
