The details

Booking Information

A few helpful details about securing your date and what to expect when you book The Good Intentions.

Making it official

  1. Get in touch. Send us your date, venue, event details and the kind of lineup you're considering.
  2. Receive your tailored quote. We'll confirm availability, performance arrangements and pricing.
  3. Secure your date. A 10% booking deposit is required to confirm the booking. The remaining balance is due 14 days before the event.
  4. Enjoy the day. We'll work with you and your venue to make sure the music fits smoothly into your event.

Performance details

Ceremony

Music for guest arrival, the key ceremony moments and post-ceremony celebrations. Song requests can be discussed in advance.

Cocktail hour

Approximately 90 minutes of live music, creating an easy-going atmosphere while guests mingle.

Reception

Three sets of approximately 45–50 minutes, with breaks between sets. We can also provide background/DJ music between performances.

Sound, lighting & setup

The Good Intentions provide the PA and performance lighting, and manage the sound for the band's performance. We'll confirm access, setup space, power and timing requirements with your venue ahead of the event.

If your event requires separate sound systems in different areas, additional equipment, unusual room changes or special production, we'll discuss any extra costs in your quote.

Travel & meals

  • Standard metropolitan Melbourne travel is included in the quoted package unless otherwise specified.
  • Regional and interstate events are quoted individually, including any applicable travel and accommodation requirements.
  • A meal must be provided for each musician performing at the reception.

Extra time & special requests

Additional performance time, extended sets, special song preparation and changes to the agreed lineup can be discussed in advance and confirmed in writing as part of your booking.

Your booking confirmation and quote will set out the exact lineup, timings, inclusions, venue arrangements and final price for your event.
